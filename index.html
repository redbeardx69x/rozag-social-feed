RoZAG Social Feed

A community tool designed to help participating creators share their latest public social content with the RoZAG community in one place.

See how it works

Creator-first

Creators choose whether to connect an account. The service is intended to work only with content and account data that the creator has authorized the application to access.

Community sharing

New public content can be surfaced to the community feed so members can discover videos without manually checking every creator's profile.

Simple & focused

The project is built around content discovery and community sharing. It does not sell creator data or attempt to modify creator content.

What this service does

RoZAG Social Feed is an independent community project. Participating creators can authorize supported social integrations so the service can retrieve permitted public content and share links with the RoZAG community.

Third-party services: TikTok, YouTube, Twitch and Discord are independent services. RoZAG Social Feed is not owned, operated, endorsed, or sponsored by them.
