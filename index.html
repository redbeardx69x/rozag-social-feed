Everything in one Social Hub

A single Discord app for community social-feed automation.

  • Automatic Social Hub setup
  • Slash-command management
  • Creator-to-Discord member linking
  • Private TikTok authorization DMs
  • Per-server feed delivery
  • Creator Watch — follow external creators without requiring Discord membership
  • Platform roadmap with Coming Soon integrations

Simple administration

Server admins manage the Social Hub directly from Discord. RoZAG handles the platform-side plumbing in the background.

  • /setup_social_hub — create or repair the Social Hub
  • /social add — add a creator
  • /social list — view connected accounts
  • /social remove — remove an account
  • /creator watch — follow an external creator
  • /creator list — list watched creators
  • /creator unwatch — unfollow a creator

Authorization: Kick and TikTok creators complete their platform authorization through RoZAG.
