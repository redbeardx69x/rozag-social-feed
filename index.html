Made for RoZAG

RoZAG Social Hub is a multi-server Discord application built to automate creator feeds without making server admins manage separate platform bots.

  • Automatic Social Hub and platform-channel setup
  • YouTube, TikTok and Twitch feeds in one Discord app
  • Creator Watch for creators who are not Discord members
  • Per-server feed delivery and independent creator connections
  • TikTok authorization through the official OAuth flow
  • Twitch live detection with EventSub plus a 60-second delivery safety net
  • Instagram, Kick and X / Twitter planned as future integrations

Simple administration

Server admins manage the Social Hub directly from Discord. RoZAG handles the platform-side plumbing in the background.

  • /setup_social_hub — create or repair the Social Hub
  • /social add — add a YouTube, TikTok or Twitch creator
  • /social list — view connected accounts
  • /social remove — remove an account
  • /creator watch — follow an external creator without requiring Discord membership
  • /creator list — view Creator Watch entries
  • /creator unwatch — stop watching a creator
