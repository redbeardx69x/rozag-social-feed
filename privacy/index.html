ROZAG SOCIAL HUB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

RoZAG Social Hub is designed to use only the information required to provide its Discord social-feed functionality.

1. Information used

Depending on the connected platform, RoZAG may store identifiers, usernames, channel information, authorization tokens and configuration needed to monitor an account and deliver permitted content.

2. Authorization data

Platform authorization is performed through the relevant platform's official OAuth system. Sensitive authorization credentials are intended for server-side use and are not published on the website.

3. Discord information

The bot may store Discord server, channel and user identifiers needed to configure feeds, permissions and creator-to-member relationships.

4. Use of information

Information is used to operate the Social Hub, monitor configured accounts, deliver notifications and maintain the service. RoZAG does not sell creator data.

5. Third-party platforms

Connected services have their own privacy policies and data practices. RoZAG is not responsible for third-party platform processing outside the service.

6. Data removal

Server administrators can remove configured social accounts through the bot's administration commands. Platform authorization can also be revoked through the relevant platform account settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Rozag@coolvetspaces.com.
