Privacy Policy

RoZAG Social Hub · Effective 26 August 2026

1. What RoZAG Social Hub does

RoZAG Social Hub connects Discord servers to supported creator feeds. The application stores configuration and delivery information needed to provide those features.

2. Discord information

RoZAG may store Discord server identifiers, channel identifiers and configuration required to deliver feeds to the correct server and channel. The application also uses information supplied by Discord to operate the bot and determine server configuration.

3. Social platform information

For connected creators, RoZAG may store public creator identifiers, usernames, profile URLs, stream or post identifiers and delivery state. Where authorization is required, RoZAG stores the authorization information needed to provide the integration.

4. TikTok authorization

TikTok authorization is performed through TikTok's official OAuth flow. RoZAG does not request or store a TikTok password.

5. Website status

The website exposes a limited public service-status endpoint showing operational status, server count and heartbeat information. It is intended for service health visibility and does not expose private creator authorization data.

6. Data sharing

RoZAG does not sell personal information. Data is processed as necessary to operate the Discord application and its connected platform integrations. Third-party platforms remain subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored application data and authorization information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and removal

Configuration and delivery data may be retained while an integration or server is configured. Removing an account or server configuration may remove or deactivate associated operational data according to the application's database and maintenance processes.

9. Support

Privacy questions and support requests are handled through the RoZAG Discord Support Group.
