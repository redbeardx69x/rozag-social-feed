Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RoZAG Social Feed handles information when a participating creator connects a supported social account.

Information we may receive

Depending on the platform and permissions approved for the application, we may receive basic account information and permitted public content information, such as a creator identifier, display name, video identifier, title, publication time, thumbnail information, and public video URL.

How we use information

  • To identify the participating creator and retrieve permitted content.
  • To detect new content and prevent duplicate community posts.
  • To publish links and limited content metadata to the configured community feed.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use connected account information for unrelated advertising profiles.
  • We do not intentionally collect passwords.
  • We do not request access to private content unless a platform's approved feature specifically requires it and the creator explicitly authorizes it.

Data retention

Only information reasonably needed to operate the feed is retained. Content identifiers may be retained to prevent duplicate posts. Access tokens or other authorization credentials, where required by a platform, are stored separately from public feed content and are not displayed publicly.

Third-party platforms

When you use a supported social platform, that platform's own privacy policy and terms also apply. RoZAG Social Feed is an independent service and is not affiliated with TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, or Discord.

Your choices

A participating creator can stop using the integration by disconnecting or revoking authorization through the relevant platform where available. If you have a privacy question or request, use the contact information on our Contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
