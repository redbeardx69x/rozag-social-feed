Privacy Policy

RoZAG Social Hub · Effective 18 August 2026
Summary: RoZAG Social Hub is designed to collect and retain only the information needed to operate its Discord social-feed features and authorized social integrations.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how RoZAG Social Hub processes information when it is installed in a Discord server or when a person authorizes a supported social account.

2. Information processed

  • Discord information: Discord server/guild identifiers, channel identifiers, user/member identifiers, usernames or display information needed to associate a creator with a Discord member, and information provided through interactions with the application.
  • Social-account information: Creator names, usernames, profile URLs, platform account identifiers, and public content metadata needed to monitor a subscribed creator feed.
  • OAuth information: Where a creator authorizes a platform such as TikTok, the application may store access and refresh tokens securely on its server so it can continue performing the authorized function without requiring the creator to authorize every polling cycle.
  • Operational information: Application configuration, enabled/disabled status, timestamps, polling state, and identifiers used to prevent duplicate feed posts.

3. TikTok data

When a creator authorizes TikTok, RoZAG Social Hub uses TikTok's official authorization flow. The current integration requests user.info.basic and video.list. TikTok documents these as read-only access to basic profile information and a user's public TikTok videos. RoZAG uses that data to associate the authorized creator with the registered feed and retrieve public videos for Discord notifications.

RoZAG Social Hub does not request or store a creator's TikTok password. OAuth access and refresh tokens are handled server-side.

4. How information is used

  • To identify and manage registered social-feed accounts.
  • To send authorization instructions to the Discord member linked to a creator account.
  • To retrieve authorized public social content and publish permitted feed notifications in Discord.
  • To maintain OAuth authorization and refresh access when supported by the connected platform.
  • To prevent duplicate posts and maintain reliable feed operation.
  • To diagnose technical problems and maintain security.

5. What we do not do

RoZAG Social Hub is not designed to sell personal information, build advertising profiles, or collect social-media passwords.

The application does not intentionally scrape private social-media content. Its TikTok integration uses TikTok's official API.

6. Discord and third-party platforms

Discord, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, and their related services have their own privacy policies and data practices. RoZAG Social Hub is a separate third-party application and does not control how those platforms process information.

Our privacy policy describes RoZAG's own processing. It does not replace the privacy policy of any connected platform.

7. Sharing

Information is shared only as necessary to operate the requested integration. For example, authorized public creator content and feed metadata are sent to the configured Discord server/channel. OAuth information is used for communication with the relevant platform's API.

We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

OAuth credentials are intended to be kept server-side and are not displayed to Discord users. Access to the application server and database should be restricted to authorized operators.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for the application's operation.

9. Retention and deletion

Account configuration and integration data may be retained while a creator or server is subscribed to the relevant feed. Removing a social account or removing the application from a server is intended to stop that integration. Where stored OAuth credentials are no longer required, they should be revoked or deleted as part of account removal or maintenance.

10. Your choices

A creator can choose not to authorize a social integration. A server administrator can remove a registered social account or remove the application from the server. A creator can also revoke authorization through the relevant third-party platform where that platform provides such a control.

11. Children's privacy

RoZAG Social Hub is not intended to collect information from children for independent use. The application operates inside Discord servers and relies on Discord and connected platforms for their own age and safety controls.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, integrations, or applicable requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to RoZAG Social Hub, contact the application operator through the RoZAG Discord community or the contact method published with the application.
