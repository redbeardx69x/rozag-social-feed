About RoZAG Social Hub

RoZAG Social Hub is a multi-server Discord application focused on bringing creator activity into the communities that follow it.

Supported now

  • YouTube creator feeds
  • TikTok creator feeds with official OAuth authorization
  • Twitch live alerts through EventSub plus a 60-second safety net
  • Creator Watch for external creators who are not Discord members
  • Per-server feed delivery
