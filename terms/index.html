Terms of Service

Last updated: 27 August 2026

RoZAG Social Hub is an independent community project that connects Discord communities with supported creator-feed services.

1. Use of the service

You may use RoZAG Social Hub to connect supported social accounts and deliver permitted creator content into Discord servers where you have appropriate authorization.

2. Account authorization

When a platform requires OAuth authorization, the account owner must complete that authorization through the platform's official authorization flow. RoZAG does not ask users to provide platform passwords.

3. Third-party services

YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Kick, Discord and other third-party platforms are independent services. Their own terms, policies, availability and API limitations apply.

4. Content

RoZAG Social Hub does not claim ownership of creator content. The service primarily distributes links and permitted metadata supplied by supported platforms.

5. Availability

The service is provided on a best-effort basis. Third-party API outages, authorization changes, rate limits and other technical issues may temporarily interrupt functionality.

6. Changes

Features and supported platforms may change as APIs and the project evolve.

7. Contact

For support, contact Rozag@coolvetspaces.com or use the RoZAG support Discord.
